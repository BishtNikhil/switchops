## 💼 LinkedIn Post

**Headline:** Turning Solo AI Coding Agents into 24/7 Production Incident Teammates with Switch ⚡

AI coding tools like Claude Code, Codex, and Antigravity have revolutionized how we write software in isolation. But when a critical production outage hits, engineering is fundamentally collaborative.

For the **Flip the Switch Hackathon** hosted by **WeMakeDevs** and **DevTools Academy**, our team built **SwitchOps**: an autonomous multi-agent incident war room that connects directly into your team's real chat channels (Slack, Microsoft Teams, and Discord).

### How SwitchOps Works:
1. **Shared Switch Room:** Every teammate and agent reads from the exact same shared memory—chat logs, tasks, decisions, and runbooks.
2. **Specialized Agent Roles:**
   - **@SwitchTriager (Claude Code):** Ingests alerts, parses stack traces, and calculates blast radius.
   - **@InfraOps (Antigravity):** Scans Kubernetes pods, correlates git commits, and isolates the faulty deployment.
   - **@HotfixCoder (Codex):** Executes the rollback, runs smoke tests, and synthesizes the post-mortem.
3. **Voice-First Commanding with Wispr Flow:** Spoken incident sign-offs with cryptographic audit logging.

Switch proved that the future of developer tools is moving from isolated bots to collaborative AI teammates.
